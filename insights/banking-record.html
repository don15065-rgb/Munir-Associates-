In a banking dispute, the record matters

A useful first review looks beyond the outstanding figure to the facility, security and correspondence.

Banking & finance

A useful first review looks beyond the outstanding figure to the facility, security and correspondence.

Begin with the transaction

The central document may be a facility agreement, but it is rarely the only relevant one. Guarantees, security instruments, renewals, account statements and subsequent communications can all affect the questions in dispute.

Separate the obligations

A borrower, guarantor and security provider may have distinct legal positions. The answer depends on the actual documents and the relevant law, not a general assumption that everyone carries identical liability.

Keep the chronology intact

Drawdowns, repayments, notices and changes to facilities should be placed in sequence. A reliable chronology assists both legal analysis and the practical discussion of a dispute.

Review the available route

Forum, remedy and procedure turn on the relationship, nature of the finance and applicable statutory framework. These should be identified before litigation strategy is settled.

A note on legal information

This article is general information and not advice on a particular matter. Jurisdiction, procedure and relevant law should be verified against the facts and current legal position.
