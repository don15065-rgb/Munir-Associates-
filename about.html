OUR PRACTICE

Serious work.
Clear thinking.

Muneer & Associates advises individuals, companies and financial institutions on litigation, documents and regulatory questions.

The firm is led by Muneer Ahmed, an Advocate of the High Court with more than sixteen years of practice. Its work extends from civil, banking, criminal and customs matters to commercial arrangements, intellectual property and compliance.

Our chambers are based at Clifton Centre, Karachi. We place emphasis on a coherent documentary record, precise legal analysis and direct communication throughout an engagement.
