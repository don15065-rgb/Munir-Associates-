General information only

Material on this website is provided for general information and is not legal advice or a legal opinion on any particular situation. It should not be relied on to decide whether to begin, defend or discontinue proceedings. Laws, court practice, deadlines and regulatory requirements can change.

No advocate–client relationship

Viewing the website, sending an email, submitting an enquiry draft or using a telephone or messaging link does not, by itself, create an advocate–client relationship. The firm must first agree to act, subject to conflicts, capacity and other professional requirements.

No guarantee of outcomes

Descriptions of practice areas, experience and qualifications are factual professional information. They do not imply special accreditation, superiority to other advocates, or a promise of success in any matter.
