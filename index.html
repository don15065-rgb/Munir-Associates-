05 / OUR APPROACH

Good advocacy begins
before the argument.

No two instructions are identical. The discipline behind them should be.

01

Understand the record.

Begin with the facts, documents, procedural position and practical objective.

02

Give candid advice.

Explain strengths, weaknesses and what the available legal routes involve.

03

Prepare with care.

Keep drafting, evidence and advocacy connected to the actual issues in dispute.
