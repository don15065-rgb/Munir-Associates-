PROFILE

Clear advice begins with identifying the legal question that actually determines the matter.

Noman Sohail is an Advocate enrolled with the Sindh Bar Council. He practises across litigation, corporate advice and regulatory work, with a particular interest in banking and finance law, financial-crime compliance and intellectual property.

He holds an LLM in International Banking Law and Finance from the University of Edinburgh, awarded with Distinction, and an LLB (Hons) from the University of London. He is a Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist (CAMS).

His background includes in-house intellectual-property and commercial work in the pharmaceutical sector. His research interests include financial regulation, the structure of financial institutions and the interaction between procedure and substantive rights.
