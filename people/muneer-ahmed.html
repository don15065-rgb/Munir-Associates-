PROFILE

Litigation requires a grasp of both the law and the record on which the court must act.

Muneer Ahmed leads Muneer & Associates. His practice spans more than sixteen years across litigation and legal advisory, including civil, commercial, banking, customs and criminal matters.

His professional work includes the drafting and review of legal instruments, proceedings before the civil and banking courts, appearances before the High Courts, and matters involving specialised forums and tribunals.

His practice also encompasses arbitration and mediation, as well as advisory and documentation work for financial institutions and commercial clients.
