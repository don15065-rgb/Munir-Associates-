BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Common questions

What should I include in my first message?

Your contact details, the general nature of the matter, the city or forum involved and any approaching deadline. Avoid sending highly confidential facts or documents before an engagement and secure channel are agreed.

Does an enquiry mean the firm has agreed to act?

No. An advocate–client relationship begins only when the firm has assessed the matter, completed any required conflict checks and expressly agreed to an engagement.

Can I visit the office without an appointment?

Please call or email first so the appropriate lawyer can confirm availability.

Can you guarantee an outcome?

No. Any assessment depends on the facts, documents, applicable law and the court or other forum. No result can responsibly be guaranteed.
