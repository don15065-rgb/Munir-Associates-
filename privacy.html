How this static website treats information entered or sent through contact links.

What the website processes

This website is designed as a static informational website. The enquiry form prepares an email draft or WhatsApp message on your own device; it does not send information to, or store information on, a website database. You choose whether to send the message through your selected email or messaging provider.

External services

The website offers optional Google Maps, WhatsApp and external professional-profile links. Google Fonts may be loaded by your browser to display typography. Those providers may process data under their own privacy terms. No analytics or advertising tracking code is included in this build.

What happens after you contact us

Messages you send by email, telephone or WhatsApp may be used to respond to your enquiry, consider whether the firm can act, and manage related professional correspondence. Communication is subject to applicable professional duties and legal obligations. Do not send highly sensitive material until appropriate arrangements have been made.
